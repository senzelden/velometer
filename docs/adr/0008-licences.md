# 0008 — Licences

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01
- Extended: 2026-10-21 (M16: boundaries, population, basemap, map libraries, fonts, DB-IP; from the legal review `docs/review/m16-legal.md`, fixes F1-F8 and F15)

## Decision

- **Code:** MIT (`LICENSE`).
- **Derived data:** ODbL 1.0, with the attribution "© OpenStreetMap contributors". This covers our tier lengths, metrics, exports and the app's CSV. BikeNetKit (Zenodo 22271229) states no licence; its files are OSM extracts and therefore under ODbL by share-alike. We use single figures from it, cited with the DOI.
- **External comparison numbers** are stored with source URL and retrieval date. Examples: Clean Cities, Copenhagenize, QECIO (no licence stated) and official city figures. They are cited, not redistributed beyond what citation allows.
- **Test fixtures** containing real OSM data (the Berlin golden set) are ODbL. Each carries an attribution note.
- **M7 narratives and quotations:** narratives quote external sources only, as short excerpts (at most 60 words) with attribution, publisher, title, date and link. Full texts are never stored. Excerpts and their English glosses live in `content/sources/*.yaml`; project documents are cited as data, not quoted.
- **M8 open data (counters, the Berlin plan network):** each downloaded dataset records its URL, licence and retrieval time beside the raw file under `data/` (git-ignored, never committed), and every page that shows it carries an attribution line. Licences in use: **dl-de-zero-2.0** (Berlin counter file; the Senate's WFS layers `radverkehrsnetz` and `radverkehrsmassnahmen`, read for the plan overlay and the infraVelo completed projects), **ODbL** (Paris counters, with the same share-alike obligations as our derived data), **CC BY 4.0** (Vienna, Helsinki, Madrid and Barcelona counters; attribution to the publisher on each Counts tab) and **NLOD** (Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data: Oslo's state counters from Statens vegvesen; attribution required). The **Licence Ouverte** (Etalab) of the French portals checked for Lyon, Bordeaux and Strasbourg is not used: none of their counter series was loaded. The stored index values and daily totals are derived from these datasets and keep their attribution. Plan documents (Verordnungen, Drucksachen, Hauptausschuss reports, other cities' plans) state no licence; they are stored as printed figures with a citation, never redistributed as files.
- **M16 OSM notice in downloads:** every download file carries one notice with both URIs (ODbL 1.0 §4.2 b): "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)". It is the CSV preamble line, the top-level `attribution` member of the tier-map and change-map GeoJSON and of `series.json`. Images (tier-map PNG footers, chart downloads) print the same notice with the addresses written out without the scheme (`openstreetmap.org/copyright`, `opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/`), so no page names a host it does not load from. Pages keep the linked credit of the footer and the map corner.
- **M16 administrative boundaries (Eurostat GISCO):** the LAU 2024 and FUA boundaries are GISCO data. GISCO grants their use on condition that the data are not used for commercial purposes; commercial use needs a licence from EuroGeographics. velometer and the Datalab stay non-commercial (no ads, no paid service, no sale of data); a company operator or any paid offer needs that licence, or a lawyer's answer, first (legal review 1.2b, a decision for the user). The notice is GISCO's prescribed text, word for word: EN "© EuroGeographics for the administrative boundaries", DE "© EuroGeographics bezüglich der Verwaltungsgrenzen". It stands in the tier map's attribution (added to the map itself, independent of the tile layer), the site footer, the tier-map PNG footer, the tier-map GeoJSON `attribution`, every CSV preamble and the methodology's licences section. The GISCO outline in a tier-map GeoJSON (its `boundary` member) is not ODbL: the file says so in `boundary_note`, and the file is a collective database under ODbL §4.5 a.
- **M16 population:** **Eurostat** (GISCO LAU 2024 `POP_2024`, `demo_r_pjangrp3`): reuse is authorised with the source acknowledged; a modification must be stated and a non-responsibility disclaimer included. **INSEE** (Lyon, Strasbourg, Bordeaux; Licence Ouverte 2.0): credited "Source : Insee" with the millésime ("populations de référence 2023"). **INE** (Madrid, Barcelona; CC BY 4.0): credited "Elaboración propia con datos extraídos del sitio web del INE: www.ine.es", since per-capita figures are a processing. Each CSV preamble names the sources stored on the runs of its rows (`runs.population_source`), so a per-city file names only its own; the methodology names each credit with its cities. Both add: "Per-capita figures are computed by velometer; Eurostat, INSEE and INE bear no responsibility for them."
- **M16 counter credit (CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B), and the other counter licences alike):** what the Counts tab and the compare chart show is an index and totals we compute, so the counter credit ends "; index and totals computed by velometer" (DE "; Index und Summen von velometer berechnet").
- **M16 basemap (Protomaps):** velometer uses a Protomaps basemap extract, served from its own host for its own maps only. The tiles are a Produced Work of OSM under ODbL, so the map credit names "© OpenStreetMap contributors", linked to the copyright page, and "Protomaps". The `landcover` layer (derived from ESA WorldCover, CC BY 4.0) is not drawn; if it ever is, the credit adds "ESA WorldCover (CC BY 4.0)". The extract is not offered as a tile service and not under the Protomaps name.
- **M16 map libraries:** Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause) and protomaps-leaflet (BSD-3-Clause) are vendored. Each keeps its header comment in the shipped file, has its licence file (`leaflet.LICENSE`, `protomaps-leaflet.LICENSE`) exported beside it, and a `static/vendor/VERSIONS` entry.
- **M16 fonts (Datalab page):** Martian Mono and Public Sans, both SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL), are self-hosted as woff2, with each family's `OFL.txt` (and its copyright line) published beside the files. Neither declares a Reserved Font Name, so subsets keep their names. No font is loaded from another host.
- **M16 DB-IP Lite Country (CC BY 4.0):** the access statistics derive a visitor's country offline, on the server, from the masked address. The statistics report is internal, and it carries the credit "IP Geolocation by DB-IP (https://db-ip.com), CC BY 4.0" all the same.
